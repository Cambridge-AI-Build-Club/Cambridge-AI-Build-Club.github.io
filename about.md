The **Cambridge AI Builder Club** brings students together to explore what they can make with artificial intelligence. We are student-led and work in collaboration with Claude.

You can start with a question, an unfinished idea or a project already in progress. Students from every subject and level of experience are welcome.

Our **workshops** offer hands-on practice, **hackathons** make space to build in a team, and **demos** give builders a chance to share their work. Across these formats, the aim is simple: try ideas, learn from one another and make something you can show.

Explore our [activities](/events/) to find a format that interests you, or check the [calendar](/calendar/) for session details.

## Part of a wider builder community

Our activities start in Cambridge, but the community reaches beyond it. We are part of the **Claude Builder Club** programme, which supports student-led AI communities at universities. It connects our local building sessions with people exploring similar ideas on other campuses.

**Claude Campus Ambassadors** are student organisers who help their campuses explore AI through workshops, events and conversations, with support from Anthropic. Learn more about their role in the [Claude campus programme](https://claude.com/programs/campus).

We wanted to make those connections easier to find. That's why we built **CBC World**: a way to discover campus ambassadors, find people at other universities and start a conversation beyond your own club.
